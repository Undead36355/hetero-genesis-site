02The studio工作室

Independent by design.以独立方式做游戏。

Tianyang Studio is an independent game studio. Design, systems and art stay in the same hands, so the creature on screen and the rules underneath it are built together.天阳工作室是一家独立游戏工作室。设计、系统与美术由同一双手完成，所以屏幕上看到的身体与它底下的规则一起生长。

Our games start from the body: how it moves, what it eats, what it can lose, and what it can become. The ocean is not a backdrop — it is a system that pushes back.我们的游戏从身体出发：它怎么移动、吃什么、会失去什么，又能变成什么。海洋从来不是背景，而是一套会反击的系统。

01

Mechanics you can see看得见的机制

Supply, space and contact are shown in the body itself, not hidden in a spreadsheet.供给、空间与接触都表现在身体上，而不是藏在数值表里。

02

Anatomy, drawn by hand手绘的身体

Layered tissue, ink lines and real silhouettes — the creature stays readable at play distance.分层组织、墨线与真实轮廓——生物在正常游戏视距下依然可读。

03

Oceans that push back会反击的海洋

Light, terrain and ecology change what is safe, what is worth reaching, and when to leave.光照、地形与生态决定哪里安全、哪里值得去，以及什么时候该离开。
