02 / 工作室The studio

小小的工作室，
向未知的深处。A small studio.
A deeper kind
of exploration.

天阳工作室专注独立游戏。我们的创作从一个问题开始：如果身体不只是角色的外形，而是你亲手搭建的一套生命系统，会发生什么？Tianyang Studio makes independent games. We start with a question: what if a body were more than a character’s shape — what if it were a living system you could build yourself?

TIANYANG STUDIOBODIES & OCEANS
01

身体，是玩法的起点。The body comes first.

形态、器官与连接共同改变行动方式。每次构筑，都在回答一个新的生存问题。Shape, organs and connections change how you play. Each build is a new answer to the question of survival.

02

系统，要能被看见。Systems you can see.

让空间、接触与供给呈现在身体上，让选择产生清晰可读的变化。Space, contact and supply show up in the body itself, so your decisions make a visible difference.

03

海洋，不只是背景。An ocean that answers.

把光照、地形和生态放进探索，让每次下潜都有值得观察、尝试与适应的事。Light, terrain and ecology shape exploration. Every dive gives you something to observe, try and adapt to.
