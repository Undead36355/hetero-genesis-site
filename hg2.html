The game关于游戏

One connected body.
No fixed slots.一具连通的身体，
没有固定槽位。

FORM / FLOW / LIFE

You begin with a living body and reshape it. Outlines are drawn directly. Organs take up real space. Pipes become a working network with multiple sources, branches and loops. Nothing is slotted into a preset socket, and nothing works without supply.从一具活着的身体开始，你直接改变它的轮廓。器官占据真实空间，管道连接成一张可多源、可分支、可成环的功能网络。没有预设槽位，也没有脱离供给的器官。

Out in the ocean you forage for nutrients and genes, return to safe water to reproduce and redesign the next generation, then prepare a guarded route and migrate. The journey ends when your species can survive the destination — or when it cannot.在开放海域中，你获取营养与基因，回到安全水域繁衍并改造下一代，再准备一条受守护的通道完成迁徙。旅程的终点，取决于你的物种能否适应目的地——或者不能。

Six base body plans are starting points, not classes. Every outline, organ and connection can be rebuilt around the way you want to survive.六类基础形态只是起点，不是职业。每一道轮廓、每一个器官和每一条连接，都可以围绕你想要的生存方式重新搭建。
